AEGIS TECHNOLOGIES — VENDOR RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY
Effective: January 15, 2024 | Version: 3.0
Classification: Confidential — Procurement, Security, Legal
Owner: CISO & VP Procurement | Review: Annual

1. PURPOSE
Establish a risk-based framework for assessing, onboarding, monitoring, and offboarding
third-party vendors, suppliers, and service providers that access Aegis Technologies
data, systems, or facilities.

2. SCOPE
Applies to: All vendors with access to Aegis data (any classification), systems
(network, cloud, applications), personnel (onsite/remote), or facilities.
Excludes: Public cloud providers (AWS/GCP/Azure) — covered by Cloud Security Policy.

3. VENDOR TIERING (Risk-Based)
TIER 1 — CRITICAL (High Risk)
- Access to Restricted/Confidential data OR production systems OR > 10,000 records
- Examples: Core SaaS (Okta, GitHub, Splunk, CrowdStrike), MSSP, payroll/HRIS,
  cloud security tools, customer support platforms with data access
- Assessment: Full SIG Lite + custom questionnaire, SOC 2 Type II + bridge letter,
  penetration test (annual), on-site assessment (if critical), financial review
- Contract: DPA/BAA mandatory, right-to-audit, 30-day breach notification,
  liability caps ≥ 2x contract value, termination for cause
- Monitoring: Continuous (security ratings), quarterly business reviews,
  annual re-assessment, incident notification < 4 hours

TIER 2 — HIGH (Medium-High Risk)
- Access to Internal data OR non-production systems OR 1,000–10,000 records
- Examples: Marketing tools, analytics platforms, ATS, learning management,
  video conferencing, project management, code quality tools
- Assessment: SIG Lite or CAIQ, SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001, vulnerability scan
- Contract: DPA required, breach notification 24 hours, standard liability
- Monitoring: Semi-annual review, annual re-assessment, security ratings watch

TIER 3 — MODERATE (Medium Risk)
- Access to Public data only OR no system access OR < 1,000 records
- Examples: Office supplies, catering, facilities maintenance, travel agencies,
  background check providers, swag vendors
- Assessment: Basic questionnaire, insurance verification, references
- Contract: Standard terms, data protection addendum if any data access
- Monitoring: Annual review, incident notification 72 hours

TIER 4 — LOW (Low Risk)
- No data access, no system access, no facility access beyond reception
- Examples: Utility providers, government agencies, public registries
- Assessment: None required (public entity) or basic verification
- Contract: Standard purchase order terms
- Monitoring: None (reactive only)

4. ONBOARDING PROCESS
Step 1: Requestor submits Vendor Request Form (ServiceNow) with:
  - Business justification, data classification, system access requirements
  - Estimated annual spend, contract term, renewal dates
Step 2: Procurement assigns tier (Security validates)
Step 3: Security sends assessment package (questionnaire + evidence requests)
Step 4: Vendor completes assessment (SLA: 10 business days)
Step 5: Security analyzes, scores (0–100), documents findings & residual risks
Step 6: Risk acceptance: Tier 1/2 residual risks → CISO; Tier 3 → Security Director
Step 7: Legal negotiates contract (security clauses per tier)
Step 8: Procurement executes, Finance sets up vendor record, IT provisions access
Step 9: Vendor added to vendor register (centralized, Confluence + ServiceNow)

6. CONTINUOUS MONITORING
- Security Ratings: BitSight / SecurityScorecard (daily alerts for grade changes)
- Threat Intelligence: Vendor mentions in feeds, breach databases, dark web
- Vulnerability Scanning: External scan monthly (Tier 1), quarterly (Tier 2)
- Questionnaire Refresh: Annual (Tier 1), Biennial (Tier 2), Triennial (Tier 3)
- Financial Health: Dun & Bradstreet monitoring (Tier 1/2)
- Contractual: Renewal trigger → re-assessment 90 days prior

7. INCIDENT MANAGEMENT
- Vendor breach notification: Tier 1 = 4 hrs, Tier 2 = 24 hrs, Tier 3 = 72 hrs
- Aegis IR team engages: Joint investigation, evidence preservation, impact assessment
- Contractual remedies: SLA credits, termination rights, indemnification
- Post-incident: Root cause shared, corrective action plan, re-assessment triggered

8. OFFBOARDING
- Trigger: Contract expiry, termination, vendor exit, scope reduction
- Process: Access revocation (IAM, VPN, physical, API), data return/destruction
  certification, subprocessor notification, final security review
- Timing: Immediate for cause, 30 days for normal expiry
- Evidence: Offboarding checklist signed by Requestor, IT, Security, Procurement

9. FOURTH-PARTY / SUPPLY CHAIN RISK
- Tier 1 vendors: Subprocessor inventory required, flow-down clauses mandatory
- Critical dependencies: Map to Aegis services, assess concentration risk
- Software supply chain: SBOM requested for on-prem software, SLSA for build
- Open source: Not vendor-managed — covered by Dependency Management Policy

10. EXCEPTIONS & WAIVERS
- Business-critical vendor failing minimum controls
- Documented compensating controls (network segmentation, enhanced monitoring, DLP)
- CISO approval (Tier 1/2), Security Director (Tier 3), max 180 days
- Quarterly re-evaluation, exit strategy required

11. METRICS & REPORTING
- Vendor count by tier, assessment completion rate, overdue assessments
- High/critical findings open > 90 days, risk acceptances active
- Incident count by vendor tier, mean time to notify
- Spend by tier, contract renewal pipeline (90-day view)
- Reported monthly to CISO, quarterly to Audit Committee

12. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES
- Requestor: Business case, data classification, requirements, relationship owner
- Procurement: Process execution, contract negotiation, vendor register maintenance
- Security: Tiering, assessment, scoring, monitoring, incident coordination
- Legal: Contract review, DPA/BAA, regulatory compliance, dispute resolution
- Finance: Payment terms, financial health, tax compliance, spend tracking
- IT: Access provisioning/revocation, technical integration, monitoring config
