AEGIS TECHNOLOGIES — TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
Effective: April 1, 2024 | Version: 2.2
Classification: Internal — All Personnel
Owner: Controller | Review: Annual

1. PURPOSE
Establish clear guidelines for business travel, expense reimbursement, and corporate
card usage to ensure responsible spending, compliance, and audit readiness.

2. GENERAL PRINCIPLES
- Spend company money as if it were your own
- Prioritize virtual meetings; travel only when in-person drives clear business outcome
- Book early, choose cost-effective options, submit expenses within 5 business days
- Receipts required for all expenses ≥ $25 (digital photos accepted via Expensify/Concur)
- Non-reimbursable: Alcohol (except hosted customer dinners, max 2 drinks/person),
  personal entertainment, fines/penalties, political contributions, gifts > $150

3. TRAVEL AUTHORIZATION
- Domestic: Manager approval via Concur (pre-trip), auto-approved ≤ $2,000
- International: Director+ approval, CISO notification for high-risk countries
- Customer-facing: Pre-approved via opportunity record (Salesforce), no separate request
- Conferences: VP approval if > $3,000 total cost, speaking slots = auto-approved
- Emergency: Verbal approval + Concur within 24 hrs

4. TRANSPORTATION
Air:
- Domestic: Economy/Coach (Comfort+/Main Cabin Extra allowed for flights > 4 hrs)
- International: Premium Economy (flights > 8 hrs), Business (flights > 12 hrs or red-eye)
- Upgrades: Personal funds/miles only, no company reimbursement
- Baggage: 1 checked bag reimbursed, 2nd if trip > 7 days
- Booking: Concur Travel (preferred), 14-day advance purchase target
- Changes/Cancellations: Change fees reimbursed if business-driven

Ground:
- Rideshare/Taxi: Standard (UberX/Lyft), Black/XL only if 3+ passengers or late night (10pm–6am)
- Rental Car: Compact/Intermediate, insurance via corporate card (decline rental co. coverage)
- Parking: Airport (economy), hotel, client site — receipts required
- Public Transit: Encouraged, fully reimbursed (receipt or card tap record)
- Mileage: $0.67/mi (IRS 2024 rate) for personal vehicle, log required (date, purpose, miles)

5. LODGING
- Standard: Business-class hotels (Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, IHG brands), ≤ $300/night (major metros)
- High-cost cities (NYC, SF, DC, London, Singapore): ≤ $400/night with Director approval
- Conference hotels: Contracted rate honored even if above cap
- Extended stay (> 14 nights): Corporate housing / extended-stay preferred (cost savings)
- Loyalty: Personal accounts allowed, points accrue to employee
- Incidentals: Personal charges (minibar, movies, spa) not reimbursed

6. MEALS & ENTERTAINMENT
Per Diem (GSA rates, no receipts needed for ≤ per diem):
- Continental US: $59–$79/day (by location) — Breakfast $15, Lunch $18, Dinner $26, Incidental $5
- OCONUS: GSA foreign rates apply
- Receipt required if actuals > per diem (itemized, attendees, business purpose)

Customer/Prospect Meals:
- Business purpose documented (deal name, attendees, topics discussed)
- Limit: $150/person (food + beverage), alcohol ≤ 2 drinks/person
- Host: Most senior Aegis attendee pays (corporate card)
- Reciprocal: If vendor hosts, document and reciprocate within 90 days

Team Meals:
- Team building: $75/person quarterly (manager corporate card)
- Celebration: $50/person (project launch, milestone, holiday)
- Remote team meetup: $100/person/day (semi-annual, Director approval)

7. CORPORATE CARD (Issued to: Directors+, Sales, CS, Procurement, Travel Coordinators)
- Personal use: Strictly prohibited (immediate revocation + repayment)
- Monthly reconciliation: Required by 5th business day (Concur)
- Lost/Stolen: Call bank immediately + notify Controller (24/7 line on back of card)
- Limits: Default $10K/mo, temporary increases via Controller (business justification)
- International: No foreign transaction fees, notify bank of travel dates

8. SPECIAL CATEGORIES
Training/Conferences:
- Registration: Pre-approved, early-bird pricing targeted
- Materials: Books, courses, certifications — $3,000/yr cap (see Learning Policy)
- Travel: Covered per this policy

Home Office / Remote Work:
- Equipment: Covered by Remote Work Policy (not T&E)
- Internet: $100/mo reimbursement (Remote Work Policy)
- Co-working: $500/mo cap (Remote Work Policy)

Relocation:
- Policy separate (Relocation Policy POL-HR-015), VP People + CFO approval
- Temporary housing: 30 days max, then permanent housing expected

9. EXPENSE SUBMISSION & REIMBURSEMENT
- Deadline: 5 business days post-trip / post-purchase
- Concur: All expenses (receipts attached, attendees tagged, business purpose)
- Approval chain: Employee → Manager → Controller (auto > $500) → Finance
- Reimbursement: ACH within 10 business days of approval (payroll cycle)
- Corporate card: Paid directly by company, employee reconciles (no OOP)

10. AUDIT & COMPLIANCE
- Monthly: Controller reviews 100% of expenses > $500, 20% random sample
- Quarterly: External audit sample (Big 4), findings reported to Audit Committee
- Violations: First = warning + repayment, Second = card suspension, Third = termination
- IRS Accountable Plan: All reimbursements qualify (business connection, substantiation,
  return of excess within 120 days) — no taxable income to employee

11. INTERNATIONAL CONSIDERATIONS
- Visa: HR/Immigration coordinates (lead time 8–12 weeks)
- Health: International SOS membership (all travelers), vaccinations reimbursed
- Security: CISO briefing for high-risk, tracking app (Sitata) required
- Currency: Corporate card preferred, cash advance rare (Controller approval, receipts)
- Tax: Track days by jurisdiction (183-day rule), HR monitors

12. EXCEPTIONS
- Medical/Accessibility: HR accommodates (no approval chain, documented)
- Executive: C-suite travel per employment agreement (Controller still audits)
- Crisis/Emergency: CFO authorization, retrospective documentation within 5 days
