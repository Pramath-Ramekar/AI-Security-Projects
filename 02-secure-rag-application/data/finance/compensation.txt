Maximum payout capped at 200% of target (company × individual)

PAY EQUITY & COMPLIANCE
- Annual pay equity analysis (gender, race/ethnicity, age) by third party
- 2023 result: No statistically significant unexplained gaps (>5%)
- State/local compliance: CA/CO/NY pay transparency postings maintained
- Federal contractor: OFCCP AAP updated annually, VEVRAA/Section 503 compliant
- Section 162(m): Deductibility limits monitored for covered employees

OFF-CYCLE ADJUSTMENTS
Market Adjustment: Data-driven, CFO + VP People approval, effective next pay period
Promotion: Base + equity effective date of title change, retroactive if > 30 days
Retention: Rare, documented business case, Board Comp Committee for > $100K total
Cost of Living: Reviewed annually for high-inflation geos (>8% CYOY)

COMMUNICATION TIMELINE
- March 1: Merit/bonus/equity letters distributed via Workday
- March 15: Manager calibration sessions complete
- March 31: All adjustments effective in payroll
- Quarterly: Equity vesting statements, ESPP enrollment windows
- Annual: Total Rewards Statements (October), Benefits Open Enrollment (November)

GOVERNANCE
Compensation Committee (Board): Approves C-suite, M4, equity pool increases
CFO + VP People: Approve all L5+, M3+, off-cycle > $25K, spot grants > $50K
People Managers: Recommend within band, document business rationale
