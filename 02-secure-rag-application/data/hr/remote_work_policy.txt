AEGIS TECHNOLOGIES — REMOTE WORK & EQUIPMENT POLICY
Effective: February 1, 2024 | Version: 1.8
Classification: Internal — All Personnel
Owner: VP People Operations & CISO

1. PURPOSE
Define eligibility, expectations, security requirements, and equipment provisions for
remote and hybrid work arrangements at Aegis Technologies.

2. ELIGIBILITY
- All RFT/RPT employees eligible after 90-day probationary period
- FTC/Intern: Manager discretion, max 2 days/week
- Roles requiring on-site: Data center ops, hardware lab, physical security (designated)
- International remote: Requires VP + Legal approval (tax, compliance, export control)

3. HYBRID SCHEDULE
- Standard: 3 days on-site (Tue/Wed/Thu core), 2 days remote (Mon/Fri)
- Team agreements: Documented in Team Charter, approved by Director
- Full remote: Exception-only (medical, relocation, critical talent), VP + CISO approval
- "Anchor weeks": Quarterly all-hands week (mandatory on-site Tue–Thu)

4. REMOTE WORK EXPECTATIONS
- Core hours: 10:00–15:00 ET (overlap for all time zones)
- Availability: Slack/Calendar updated, camera-on for collaborative meetings
- Workspace: Ergonomic, private, secure (no public cafe work for Confidential+ data)
- Internet: Minimum 50 Mbps down / 10 Mbps up, wired preferred, backup hotspot
- Time zones: Hiring limited to ET ± 3 hours (AMER), GMT ± 2 (EMEA), SGT ± 2 (APAC)

5. EQUIPMENT PROVISIONING
Standard Kit (issued Day 1):
- Laptop: MacBook Pro 16" (M3 Max, 64GB) or Dell Precision 7680 (i9, 64GB)
- Monitor: 27" 4K USB-C (Dell U2723QE) × 2
- Dock: CalDigit TS4 / Dell WD22TB4
- Input: MX Keys + MX Master 3S / ergonomic alt on request
- Audio: Jabra Evolve2 85 (ANC), Logitech Brio 4K webcam
- Security: YubiKey 5C NFC (2x), privacy screen, laptop lock

Refresh Cycle: 3 years (laptop), 5 years (monitor/dock), as-needed (accessories)
Return: Within 5 business days of separation (prepaid label provided)
Damage/Loss: First incident covered, subsequent = 50% cost share (neglect = 100%)

6. SECURITY REQUIREMENTS (Remote)
- MDM enrollment mandatory (Jamf / Intune), device compliance checked continuously
- Always-on VPN (Tailscale) or ZTNA (Cloudflare Access) for corporate resources
- No Confidential/Restricted data on personal devices (clipboard, downloads, screenshots)
- Screen lock: 5 min idle, immediate on lid close / Win+L / Cmd+Ctrl+Q
- Household: No work discussions in earshot, no family use of corporate devices
- Travel: Loaner device for high-risk countries (China, Russia, Iran, NK, Cuba), CISO pre-approval

7. CO-WORKING & OFFICE STIPEND
- Co-working: WeWork All Access / local equivalent (company-paid, max $500/mo)
- Home office stipend: $1,500 one-time (desk, chair, lighting, cable mgmt)
- Monthly: $100 internet/utility reimbursement (receipts quarterly)
- Ergonomic assessment: Virtual (annual), in-person (on request)

8. TAX & COMPLIANCE
- Employee responsible for home office tax implications (consult tax advisor)
- State/country registration: HR must know primary work location at all times
- Permanent relocation: 30-day notice, new hire paperwork if new jurisdiction
- International: Maximum 30 consecutive days without entity setup (tax residency risk)

9. MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES
- Set clear outcomes (OKRs), not hours monitoring
- Weekly 1:1 (30 min), bi-weekly team sync, monthly in-person if hybrid
- Proximity bias prevention: Equal opportunity for projects, visibility, promotion
- Equipment approvals, stipend reimbursements, security compliance checks

10. EXCEPTIONS & ACCOMMODATIONS
- ADA/medical: Interactive process with HR, reasonable accommodation prioritized
- Temporary: Caregiving, medical recovery, home repair (up to 4 weeks, manager approval)
- Emergency: Natural disaster, civil unrest, pandemic — CISO declares, policy suspended
