EMPLOYMENT CLASSIFICATIONS
- Regular Full-Time (RFT): ≥ 37.5 hrs/week, benefits-eligible Day 1
- Regular Part-Time (RPT): 20–37.5 hrs/week, pro-rated benefits
- Fixed-Term Contract (FTC): Project-based, max 24 months
- Intern/Co-op: Academic partnership programs, 10–12 weeks

WORK HOURS & FLEXIBILITY
- Core hours: 10:00–15:00 local time, Monday–Friday
- Flexible start: 07:00–10:00 | Flexible end: 15:00–19:00
- Remote work: Up to 3 days/week (team-dependent, manager approval)
- "Focus Fridays": No-meeting blocks 09:00–13:00 for deep work

COMPENSATION PHILOSOPHY
- Target 65th percentile of relevant market (Radford/Levels.fyi benchmarks)
- Annual merit cycle: Effective March 1, retroactive to Jan 1
- Equity refresh: Annual grants vesting 4-year quarterly
- Spot bonuses: Up to 5% base for exceptional contributions

BENEFITS OVERVIEW
- Health: 3 medical plans (PPO, HDHP/HSA, Kaiser), 100% employee premium paid
- Dental/Vision: 100% premium paid for employee + dependents
- 401(k): 6% automatic match, immediate vesting, Roth option
- HSA: Company contributes $1,500/yr (individual) / $3,000 (family)
- Mental Health: 12 free therapy sessions/yr via Lyra, Crisis Text Line
- Learning: $3,000/yr tuition reimbursement, O'Reilly/Pluralsight access
- Parental: 16/4 weeks paid (see Leave Policy), $500 "welcome baby" stipend
- Commuter: $300/mo pre-tax transit/parking, EV charging on-site

CODE OF CONDUCT
- Zero tolerance: Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, workplace violence
- Conflicts of interest: Disclose via Ethics Portal within 5 business days
- Outside employment: Requires CISO approval for security-related work
- Gifts/Entertainment: ≤ $150 value, pre-approval > $75
- Political activity: No use of company resources, no implication of endorsement

INFORMATION SECURITY
- All employees complete SecAware training within 30 days of hire, annually thereafter
- Clean desk policy: Lock screens (Win+L / Cmd+Ctrl+Q), secure physical media
- Phishing simulations: Monthly, failure = mandatory remediation (not punitive)
- Lost device: Report to IT Security within 1 hour via Slack #security-incidents
- Classification: Public / Internal / Confidential / Restricted — handle accordingly

PERFORMANCE & DEVELOPMENT
- Quarterly OKRs: Set in Workday, reviewed at Q+1 midpoint
- Career frameworks: IC (L1–L7) and Management (M1–M4) tracks
- Promotion cycles: Semi-annual (March/September), portfolio-based review
- Mentorship: Formal program matches L3+ with L1–L2, 6-month cycles

SEPARATION
- Voluntary resignation: 2 weeks notice standard, 4 weeks for Director+
- Involuntary: Severance = 2 weeks base per year of service (min 4, max 26)
- COBRA: Company subsidizes 50% for 3 months post-separation
- Alumni network: Lifetime access to job board, quarterly events
