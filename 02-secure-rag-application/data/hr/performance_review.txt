AEGIS TECHNOLOGIES — PERFORMANCE REVIEW GUIDELINES
Effective: February 1, 2024 | Version: 2.0
Classification: Internal — People Managers & Employees
Owner: VP People Operations

1. PURPOSE
Provide consistent framework for performance evaluation, feedback delivery, and
career development conversations across Aegis Technologies.

2. REVIEW CYCLES
- Mid-Year Check-In: September 1–30 (lightweight, forward-looking)
- Annual Review: March 1–31 (comprehensive, compensation-linked)
- New Hire: 90-day check-in (probationary), 6-month review (standard cycle entry)
- Promotion: Off-cycle portfolio review (see Career Framework)

3. EVALUATION DIMENSIONS (Weighted)
Impact & Results (40%): Business outcomes, project delivery, metrics movement
Technical/Functional Excellence (25%): Craft quality, innovation, best practices
Collaboration & Influence (20%): Cross-functional work, mentorship, knowledge sharing
Culture & Values Alignment (15%): Mission First, Intellectual Honesty, Continuous Mastery,
                                  Trust by Design, Collective Accountability

4. RATING SCALE (5-Point)
1 — Does Not Meet Expectations
   Consistently misses commitments, requires significant oversight, negative team impact
   → Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) within 30 days

2 — Partially Meets Expectations
   Inconsistent delivery, some strengths but notable gaps, developing in role
   → Focused development plan, monthly check-ins, re-evaluate in 6 months

3 — Meets Expectations (Baseline)
   Reliably delivers on commitments, solid craft, positive team contributor
   → Standard merit increase, eligible for refresh equity

4 — Exceeds Expectations
   Consistently exceeds commitments, elevates others, drives improvements
   → Accelerated merit, larger refresh, promotion conversation

5 — Significantly Exceeds Expectations
   Transformational impact, sets new standard, organization-wide influence
   → Top-of-band merit, significant equity, fast-track promotion review

5. CALIBRATION PROCESS
- Manager writes draft reviews (evidence-based, specific examples)
- Calibration sessions: VP People + HRBPs + functional VPs (2 days)
- Forced distribution guidance (not mandate): ~10% / 20% / 40% / 20% / 10%
- Bias checks: Recency, halo/horn, similarity, gender/race correlation analysis
- Final ratings locked in Workday, compensation letters generated

6. FEEDBACK DELIVERY
- Preparation: Manager completes review ≥ 48 hrs before conversation
- Conversation: 60–90 min, private, two-way dialogue, documented in Workday
- Written summary: Shared within 24 hrs, employee acknowledges receipt
- Disagreement: Employee may add written perspective (attached to review)
- Follow-up: 30-day check-in on development commitments

7. CAREER FRAMEWORK INTEGRATION
- IC Track (L1–L7): Scope, complexity, influence, technical mastery
- Management Track (M1–M4): Team size, org scope, business ownership, people development
- Dual-track: Equivalent levels (L5≈M2, L6≈M3, L7≈M4) — movement both ways
- Promotion criteria: Sustained performance at next level for 2+ review cycles
- Portfolio review: Artifacts demonstrating next-level behaviors (design docs, RFCs,
  incident leadership, mentorship outcomes, hiring contributions)

8. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLANS (PIP)
- Trigger: Rating 1, or sustained 2 across 2+ cycles, or specific behavioral concern
- Duration: 60–90 days, weekly check-ins, documented objectives & success criteria
- Support: Assigned mentor, training resources, reduced scope if appropriate
- Outcomes: Success (return to standard cycle), Extension (max 30 days), Separation
- Legal/HR review required before PIP initiation

9. DOCUMENTATION STANDARDS
- Evidence: Specific, dated, observable behaviors (not personality traits)
- STAR format: Situation, Task, Action, Result (quantified where possible)
- Avoid: "Good job," "Needs improvement," subjective adjectives without examples
- Include: Peer feedback (360 for M2+), project outcomes, incident contributions,
  learning investments, hiring/interviewing, community participation

10. TIMELINE (Annual Cycle)
Jan 15: Self-reflection opens (optional, encouraged)
Feb 1:  Peer feedback requests sent (360 for M2+, optional for IC)
Feb 15: Peer feedback due
Mar 1:  Manager draft reviews due
Mar 1–15: Calibration sessions
Mar 15–31: Review conversations conducted
Apr 1:  Ratings locked, compensation letters generated
Apr 15: Total Rewards Statements distributed

11. TRAINING & RESOURCES
- Manager training: "Effective Performance Conversations" (mandatory, annual)
- Employee guide: "Making the Most of Your Review" (intranet)
- Templates: Self-reflection, peer feedback, review write-up (Workday)
- Calibration norms: Published annually, bias mitigation reminders
