AEGIS TECHNOLOGIES — LEAVE POLICY
Effective: January 1, 2024 | Version: 3.2
Policy Owner: HR Operations | Classification: Internal

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines leave entitlements, accrual rates, and approval procedures for all full-time and part-time employees of Aegis Technologies ("the Company").

2. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
- Full-time employees (40 hrs/week): 20 working days per calendar year
- Part-time employees (20–39 hrs/week): Pro-rated based on FTE
- Accrual: 1.67 days per month of service
- Maximum carryover: 5 days into following calendar year (requires VP approval)

3. SICK LEAVE
- 10 working days per calendar year, non-cumulative
- Medical certificate required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days
- Extended illness: transition to Short-Term Disability after 10 days

4. PARENTAL LEAVE
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid at 100% base salary
- Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks paid at 100% base salary
- Adoption/foster care: Same entitlement as birth parents
- Must be taken within 12 months of birth/placement

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
- Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 working days
- Extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle): 3 working days
- Additional unpaid leave available upon request

6. JURY DUTY & CIVIC OBLIGATIONS
- Full pay for duration of service
- Employee must remit jury fees to Payroll
- Notify manager within 48 hours of summons receipt

7. UNPAID LEAVE OF ABSENCE
- Up to 90 days for personal reasons (requires Director+ approval)
- Benefits continuation: employee pays 100% of premiums
- Position held for first 30 days only

8. APPROVAL PROCESS
- ≤ 5 days: Manager approval via Workday
- 6–15 days: Manager + HR Business Partner approval
- > 15 days: VP People Operations approval required
- All leave must be requested ≥ 10 business days in advance (emergencies excepted)

9. HOLIDAYS (2024 Calendar)
New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day,
Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (2 days),
Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year's Eve — 12 days total

10. COMPLIANCE & RECORDS
- All leave tracked in Workday HRIS
- Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination
- Policy reviewed annually by HR Leadership Team
